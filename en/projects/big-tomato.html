BIG TOMATO

A Playful Identity for a Craft Beer Brand

A tomato symbol and a distinctive wordmark create a logo that is recognizable, memorable and a little playful.

My roleLogo Design · Concept Exploration · Application Mockups

SCOPE
Craft beer brand
ROLE
Logo design
OUTPUT
Logo concepts & application mockups
LOGOMOCKUPS

01 · LOGO CONCEPT

Life is like TOMATO.
Take away TAT. Find OMO.

Life has its TAT moments—a little tearful face—and its OMO moments of surprise. Remove T, A and T from TOMATO, and OMO remains. This wordplay gives BIG TOMATO a lighthearted personality without taking itself too seriously.

I focused on recognition, recall and a name people could easily talk about. The round tomato, green leaves and angled wordmark work together, while a vivid tomato red gives the identity a friendly, playful character.

02 · LOGO MOCKUPS

The logo in context.

Mockups show the logo on signage, glassware, coasters and other touchpoints, exploring its appearance across scales and materials.
