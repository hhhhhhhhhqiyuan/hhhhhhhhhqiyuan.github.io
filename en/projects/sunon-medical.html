EXHIBITION · 2020

Make a healthcare product range
easy to understand in context.

For SUNON’s healthcare furniture debut at a Shenzhen medical exhibition, I illustrated different healthcare settings for the exhibition graphics and extended the same visual language into a product-series animation, connecting the on-site experience with subsequent communication.

Delivered Work

From exhibition graphics to animation and brand communication.

The illustrations appeared in the exhibition, product animation and the cover of an internal brand magazine. After screening at the event, the animation was also shared through WeChat and Weibo.

My contribution: scene illustration, exhibition graphics, animation script and graphic assets, plus coordination with the animation supplier.
