BRAND EVENT · 2021

One event identity.
A milestone brought to life.

For SUNON’s 30th anniversary celebrations and the opening of its innovation park, I designed and helped implement the event’s online and on-site graphics, extending the visual identity across entrances, stages, displays, wayfinding and pre-event communication.

Delivered across the event gateway, main stage, photo backdrop, speaker and mascot displays, with extensions into park guides, gift cards, staff passes and digital countdown posters.
