BRANDING · VISUAL IDENTITY

OP GLOBAL

From Logo Design to Brand Applications

OP’s initial inspiration came from One Piece. Starting with the ideas of navigation, connection and strength, I explored two logo directions and developed the selected concept into brand applications.

My roleBrand Strategy · Logo Design · Visual Identity

SCOPE
BRAND IDENTITY · APPLICATION
ROLE
Brand strategy, logo design and visual identity.
OUTPUT

OverviewFINAL IDENTITYBrand Applications

Direction 1

Start with navigation and connection.

Direction 2

One punch. Direct and powerful.

The second direction used a fist as its central symbol. One Piece was the starting inspiration; One Punch extended the idea. Both share the initials OP, connecting adventure with strength. After selecting this direction, I developed the logo guidelines and applications.

02 · BRAND APPLICATIONS

The identity in use.

Business cards, building signage, shipping containers, vehicles and social media.
