RETAIL DISPLAY · 2020

Turn a product display into
a place to pause and explore.

For Longfor Paradise Walk’s anniversary, I designed a circular display around the Little Whale product, combining product recognition, seating trials and photo opportunities. I independently designed the installation, coordinated suppliers, followed fabrication and installation, and handled on-site photography.

Installed at the mall entrance, the display created a place to try the seating and take photos. On-site photography was also used in subsequent social content.
