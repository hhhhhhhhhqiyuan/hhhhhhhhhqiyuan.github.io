A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Nikki.

I’m a marketing and creative designer focused on brands reaching international markets. For each launch or brand campaign, I start by understanding the audience and the message. I then translate product benefits into brand visuals, web pages, email campaigns and social content, collaborating with the team through launch.

I also use AI in my everyday work: organizing information, exploring design directions, creating lifestyle imagery and assisting with frontend development. Reducing repetitive production gives me more time to compare options and review the details.
