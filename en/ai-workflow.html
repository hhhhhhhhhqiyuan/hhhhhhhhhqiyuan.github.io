01

Define the Brief

①—③

My role
Provide relevant, factual materials, with sources and permitted uses clearly identified.
AI’s role
Organize the materials, summarize key points and flag missing information or open questions.
Output
An input checklist, a summary and questions to resolve.

My role
Define the audience, context, priorities and acceptance criteria.
AI’s role
Break the goal into tasks and identify the required deliverables.
Output
A project brief and acceptance checklist.

My role
Specify brand guidelines, protected content and delivery requirements.
AI’s role
Organize the execution rules and flag constraints or conflicts for review.
Output
Production guidelines, protected elements and file requirements.
02

Create & Deliver

④—⑥

My role
Choose a representative sample and set the direction to explore.
AI’s role
Create the sample against the brief and rules, noting key decisions and open questions.
Output
A reviewable sample with brief supporting notes.

My role
Review the sample against the goals, identify specific issues and decide whether to revise or approve.
AI’s role
Turn feedback into actionable revisions and update the agreed production standards.
Output
A revision list, or an approved sample and production standards.

My role
Confirm the scope, review key versions and approve the final delivery.
AI’s role
Produce the remaining assets from the approved sample, check consistency and organize the files.
Output
Final assets, editable source files and a delivery checklist.
03

Validate & Reuse

⑦—⑧

My role
Use the work in context, collect feedback and data, and assess whether it meets the goal.
AI’s role
Help identify display issues, summarize feedback and analyze available data, stating sources and limitations.
Output
Usage checks, real feedback and follow-up improvements.

My role
Decide which approaches to retain, when they apply and what needs updating.
AI’s role
Organize templates, guidelines and lessons into reusable resources.
Output
A reusable library of templates, standards and lessons.
↳

Delivery is followed by real-world validation.Business outcomes are assessed using real feedback and data. Validated lessons inform the next collaboration.
